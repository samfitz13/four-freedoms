# Freedom-four design context

**Daybreak is the approved, app-wide design direction.** This document and
`design.md` replace the ceramic and editorial directions. The approved visual
reference remains `design-concepts/daybreak/index.html`; its example numbers
are not application data.

## Users and purpose

The operator and a small circle of friends testing a private beta. They use
Financial, Location, Time, and Health to understand their options, reflect on
their own data, and act deliberately. This is a personal instrument, not an
attention product. Preserve privacy, household boundaries, and existing jobs.

No leaderboards, engagement loops, streak-shaming, or invented progress.
Missing measurements mean missing information, not failure or a zero score.

## Personality

**Warm, spacious, quietly optimistic.** Handling personal numbers should feel
approachable, not like being audited. Use short, honest sentences. No hype,
urgency, mascots, upsells, or praise inflation.
No placeholder numbers, filler instructions, or boilerplate caveats in the UI.
Use concise labels, actual values, and actionable missing-data states.

Daybreak combines the spatial navigation of Xbox blades, the domestic warmth
of _Her_, and the depth of Liquid Glass. It should feel like a useful object
in a sunlit room, not a corporate analytics console.

## Aesthetic direction

- **Material:** one translucent workspace over a local cinematic backdrop.
  Soft rims, restrained shadows, readable panels, and capsule controls.
  Blur belongs to the shell and overlay separation, never text or data marks.
  Do not put another card around every paragraph or table row.
- **Color:** warm ivory, apricot, coral, and brown ink. Dark mode uses smoked
  glass, warm ivory text, and copper accents. Use the OKLCH semantic tokens in
  `app/globals.css`, not ad-hoc colors. Names and icons identify the freedoms;
  color is supporting information.
- **Type:** Manrope for headings, body, navigation, and important numbers.
  JetBrains Mono only for code and technical identifiers. Use sentence case,
  tabular numerals, right-aligned numeric columns, and readable labels.
- **Composition:** five stable blades, Home followed by the four freedoms.
  Distinguish the main workspace from navigation and supporting reflection.
  Use asymmetry for the home cards; keep dense records in tables and lists.
- **Motion:** brief, deliberate transitions. No looping ambient animation,
  bounce, wobble, or animated background photography.

Anti-references: generic SaaS card grids, neon finance terminals, glossy toy
controls, gradient orbs, embossed text, and decorative charts.

## Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.2 AA in both themes. Normal text needs 4.5:1 contrast against its
actual composited surface; large text and essential non-text UI need 3:1.
Keep visible focus, labels, landmarks, and semantic selected states. Aim for
44px controls. Do not shrink mobile navigation into unreadable metadata.

All five blades remain reachable on mobile. Arrow shortcuts apply only while
focus is in the blades. Existing workspace shortcuts pause during text entry
and overlays. Dialogs must contain and restore focus; menus must not clip
inside tables or lose their form ownership.

Light and dark are peers. Follow system preferences until explicitly changed.
Provide persistent Reduce glass, reduced-transparency and reduced-motion
support, opaque fallback surfaces, forced-colors support, and readable print
output. Support narrow screens and 200% text resizing without losing actions.

## Principles

1. **Warm shell, honest data.** Never reinterpret a score chart as net worth.
   Do not synthesize records or turn unknown balances into zero.
2. **Hierarchy before decoration.** One clear surface beats several nested
   containers. Keep typography and spacing responsible for most grouping.
3. **Soft material, firm affordances.** Focus, inputs, errors, and active
   states must stay distinct when blur, shadows, or animation disappear.
4. **Preserve the work.** Keep routes, calculations, imports, integrations,
   authentication, and household isolation intact.
5. **One durable system.** Reuse shared primitives across all 23 workspace
   tabs and supporting pages. Validate with actual components and clearly
   separate synthetic fixture checks from authenticated end-to-end testing.
